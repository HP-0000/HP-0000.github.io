두 함수 $f(x)$와 $g(x)$가 미분 가능할 때, $P(x) = f(x)g(x)$ 의 도함수는 다음과 같다.

$$\Big( f(x)g(x) \Big)' = f'(x)g(x) + f(x)g'(x)$$

---
## 증명

$$P'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{P(x+h) - P(x)}{h} = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h)g(x+h) - f(x)g(x)}{h}$$

$$P'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h)g(x+h) \mathbf{- f(x+h)g(x) + f(x+h)g(x)} - f(x)g(x)}{h}$$

$$P'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h)\Big(g(x+h) - g(x)\Big) + g(x)\Big(f(x+h) - f(x)\Big)}{h}$$

$$P'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \left[ f(x+h) \cdot \frac{g(x+h) - g(x)}{h} \right] + \lim_{h \to 0} \left[ g(x) \cdot \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h} \right]$$

$$P'(x) = f(x)g'(x) + g(x)f'(x)$$

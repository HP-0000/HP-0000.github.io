[벡터 공간](Public/Study/Math/Docs/공간/벡터%20공간.md)

**체 $(F, \underbrace{+}_F, \underbrace{\times}_F)$ 가 다음을 만족하고,**
- 켤레 연산자가 정의된다. $(\forall x, y \in F) \quad \overline{\overline{x}} = x, \;\; \overline{x + y} = \overline{x} + \overline{y}, \;\; \overline{x \cdot y} = \overline{x} \cdot \overline{y}$
- 부분체 $F_0 = \{ x \in F \mid \overline{x} = x \}$ 는 순서체이다.

**내적 함수 $\langle \cdot , \cdot \rangle$ 가  다음을 만족할 때**
$(\forall u, v \in V) \quad \langle u, v \rangle \in F$
###### 선형성 (Linearity)
- $(\forall u, v, w \in V) \quad \langle u \underbrace{+}_V v, w \rangle = \langle u, w \rangle \underbrace{+}_F \langle v, w \rangle$
- $(\forall c \in F, \; \forall u, v \in V) \quad \langle c \underbrace{\times}_V u, v \rangle = c \underbrace{\times}_F \langle u, v \rangle$
###### 켤레 대칭성 (Conjugate Symmetry)
- $(\forall u, v \in V) \quad \langle u, v \rangle = \overline{\langle v, u \rangle}$
###### 양의 정부호성 (Positive-definiteness)
- $(\forall v \in V) \quad \langle v, v \rangle \in F_0 \;\land\; \langle v, v \rangle \ge e_{F,+}$
- $\langle v, v \rangle = e_{F,+} \iff v = e_{V,+}$

체 $(F, \underbrace{+}_F, \underbrace{\times}_F)$  위의 벡터 공간  $(V, \underbrace{+}_V, \underbrace{\times}_V,\langle \cdot , \cdot \rangle)$ 를 내적 공간이라고 정의한다.

---
###### Norm($\|v \|$)
내적공간에서 Norm을 다음과 같의 정의한다.

$$\|v\| = \sqrt{\langle v, v \rangle}$$
$$V \longrightarrow F_0 \subseteq F$$
이는 다대일 함수이다.
###### Orthogonality($u \perp v$)
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|u \underbrace{+}_V v\|^2 &= \langle u \underbrace{+}_V v, u \underbrace{+}_V v \rangle \\
&= \langle u, u \rangle \underbrace{+}_F \langle u, v \rangle \underbrace{+}_F \langle v, u \rangle \underbrace{+}_F \langle v, v \rangle \\
&= \|u\|^2 \underbrace{+}_F 2 \underbrace{\times}_F \langle u, v \rangle \underbrace{+}_F \|v\|^2
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\|u \underbrace{-}_V v\|^2 &= \langle u \underbrace{-}_V v, u \underbrace{-}_V v \rangle \\
&= \langle u, u \rangle \underbrace{-}_F \langle u, v \rangle \underbrace{-}_F \langle v, u \rangle \underbrace{+}_F \langle v, v \rangle \\
&= \|u\|^2 \underbrace{-}_F 2 \underbrace{\times}_F \langle u, v \rangle \underbrace{+}_F \|v\|^2
\end{aligned}
$$

$$\|u \underbrace{+}_V v\|^2 \underbrace{-}_F \|u \underbrace{-}_V v\|^2 = 4 \underbrace{\times}_F \langle u, v \rangle$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\|u \underbrace{+}_V v\| = \|u \underbrace{-}_V v\| &\iff \|u \underbrace{+}_V v\|^2 \underbrace{-}_F \|u \underbrace{-}_V v\|^2 = e_{F,+} \\
&\iff 4 \underbrace{\times}_F \langle u, v \rangle = e_{F,+} \\
&\iff (e_{F,\times} \underbrace{+}_F e_{F,\times} \underbrace{+}_F e_{F,\times} \underbrace{+}_F e_{F,\times}) \underbrace{\times}_F \langle u, v \rangle = e_{F,+} \\
&\iff \langle u, v \rangle = e_{F,+}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$\|u \underbrace{+}_V v\| = \|u \underbrace{-}_V v\| =  \iff \langle u, v \rangle = e_{F,+}$$

$\langle u, v \rangle = e_{F,+}$ 상태를  직교 $u \perp v$ 로 정의한다.

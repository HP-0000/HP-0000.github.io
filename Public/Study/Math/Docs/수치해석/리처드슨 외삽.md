> 서로 다른 간격으로 계산된 두 근사값을 조합하여 더 높은 정밀도의 근사값을 얻는 기법을 말한다.


[적분의 잉여항](Public/Study/Math/Docs/미적분/적분의%20잉여항.md)

$$F(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{F^{(k)}(c)}{k!}(x - c)^k + \mathbf{\frac{1}{n!} \int_c^x (x - \tau)^n F^{(n+1)}(\tau) \, d\tau}$$

수치 해석 근사식의 오차항이 $f^{(q)}(\xi)$에 비례할 때, 
$$\mathbf{f^{(q)}(\xi_1)} \equiv \frac{f^{(q)}(c+h) + f^{(q)}(c-h)}{2}$$
이를 적분의 잉여항으로 나타낸다.
$F(x) = f^{(q)}(x)$ 
$$f^{(q)}(c + h) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{f^{(q+k)}(c)}{k!} h^k + \mathbf{\frac{1}{n!} \int_0^h (h - t)^n f^{(q+n+1)}(c + t) \, dt}$$$$f^{(q)}(c - h) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{f^{(q+k)}(c)}{k!} (-h)^k + \mathbf{\frac{1}{n!} \int_0^h (h - t)^n f^{(q+n+1)}(c - t) \, dt}$$
$$\mathbf{f^{(q)}(\xi_1)} = \mathbf{\sum_{\substack{k=0 \\ k \text{ 짝수}}}^{n} \frac{f^{(q+k)}(c)}{k!} h^k + \frac{1}{2 \cdot n!} \int_0^h (h - t)^n \Big[ f^{(q+n+1)}(c+t) + f^{(q+n+1)}(c-t) \Big] dt}$$


$$\mathbf{f^{(q)}(\xi_2)} \equiv \frac{f^{(q)}(c+\frac{h}{2}) + f^{(q)}(c-\frac{h}{2})}{2}$$

$$f^{(q)}\left(c + \frac{h}{2}\right) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{f^{(q+k)}(c)}{k!} \left(\frac{h}{2}\right)^k + \mathbf{\frac{1}{n!} \int_0^{h/2} \left(\frac{h}{2} - t\right)^n f^{(q+n+1)}(c + t) \, dt}$$
$$f^{(q)}\left(c - \frac{h}{2}\right) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{f^{(q+k)}(c)}{k!} \left(-\frac{h}{2}\right)^k + \mathbf{\frac{1}{n!} \int_0^{h/2} \left(\frac{h}{2} - t\right)^n f^{(q+n+1)}(c - t) \, dt}$$

$$\mathbf{f^{(q)}(\xi_2)} = \mathbf{\sum_{\substack{k=0 \\ k \text{ 짝수}}}^{n} \frac{f^{(q+k)}(c)}{k!} \left(\frac{h}{2}\right)^k + \frac{1}{2 \cdot n!} \int_0^{h/2} \left(\frac{h}{2} - t\right)^n \Big[ f^{(q+n+1)}(c+t) + f^{(q+n+1)}(c-t) \Big] dt}$$


$$
\begin{aligned}
f^{(q)}(\xi_1) - f^{(q)}(\xi_2) &= \mathbf{\sum_{\substack{k=2 \\ k \text{ 짝수}}}^{n} \frac{f^{(q+k)}(c)}{k!} \left( 1 - \frac{1}{2^k} \right) h^k} \\
&\quad + \mathbf{\frac{1}{2 \cdot n!} \int_0^h (h - t)^n \Big[ f^{(q+n+1)}(c+t) + f^{(q+n+1)}(c-t) \Big] dt} \\
&\quad - \mathbf{\frac{1}{2 \cdot n!} \int_0^{h/2} \left(\frac{h}{2} - t\right)^n \Big[ f^{(q+n+1)}(c+t) + f^{(q+n+1)}(c-t) \Big] dt}
\end{aligned}
$$


$\frac{f^{(q+n+1)}(c+t) + f^{(q+n+1)}(c-t)}{2} = f^{(q+n+1)}(\xi_t)$
 $0 \le t \le h$ 에서 $(h - t)^n \ge 0$ 
$0 \le t \le \frac{h}{2}$ 에서 $(\frac{h}{2} - t)^n \ge 0$ 

 적분의 가중  [평균값 정리](Public/Study/Math/Docs/미적분/평균값%20정리.md)에 의해 
$$
\begin{aligned}
f^{(q)}(\xi_1) - f^{(q)}(\xi_2) &= \mathbf{\sum_{\substack{k=2 \\ k \text{ 짝수}}}^{n} \frac{f^{(q+k)}(c)}{k!} \left( 1 - \frac{1}{2^k} \right) h^k} \\
&\quad +  
f^{(q+n+1)}(\xi_n) \times \frac{1}{n!} \left[ \int_0^h (h - t)^n \, dt - \int_0^{h/2} \left(\frac{h}{2} - t\right)^n \, dt \right]
\end{aligned}
$$
이를 정리하면 다음과 같다.
$$\mathbf{f^{(q)}(\xi_1) - f^{(q)}(\xi_2) = \sum_{\substack{k=2 \\ k \text{ 짝수}}}^{n} \frac{f^{(q+k)}(c)}{k!} h^k \left( 1 - \frac{1}{2^k} \right) + \frac{2^{n+1} - 1}{(n+1)! \cdot 2^{n+1}} \cdot h^{n+1} \cdot f^{(q+n+1)}(\xi_n^* \mid (c-h, c+h))}$$

$n = 1$  대입하면   $$\mathbf{f^{(q)}(\xi_1) - f^{(q)}(\xi_2) = \frac{3}{8} h^2 f^{(q+2)}(\xi_1^* \mid (c-h, c+h))}$$
원 함수 식의 참값을 $T$ , 근사식을 $F$  오차를 $E$ 로 두면 다음의 관계가 성립한다.
$T= F+E$

오차항이 $h^p$에 비례할 때, 
$E_h = C h^p f^{(q)}(\xi_1)$
  * $h$ 간격에서: $T = \mathbf{F(h)} + C h^p f^{(q)}(\xi_1)$
  * $\frac{h}{2}$ 간격에서: $T = \mathbf{F\left(\frac{h}{2}\right)} + \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^p C h^p f^{(q)}(\xi_2)$
이를 연립하면 
$$
T = F\left(\frac{h}{2}\right) + \frac{F\left(\frac{h}{2}\right) - F(h)}{2^p - 1} + \frac{C h^p}{2^p - 1} \Big[ f^{(q)}(\xi_2) - f^{(q)}(\xi_1) \Big]
$$


$$T \approx F\left(\frac{h}{2}\right) + \frac{F\left(\frac{h}{2}\right) - F(h)}{2^p - 1}$$
오차는 다음과 같다.
$$
\mathbf{-\frac{3C}{8(2^p - 1)} h^{p+2} f^{(q+2)}(\xi^*)} \quad \Big( \xi^* \in (c-h, c+h) \Big)
$$---

리처드슨 외삽법 확장

* $T = F(h) + C h^p f^{(q)}(\xi_1)$
* $T = F\left(\frac{h}{2}\right) + \frac{C}{2^p} h^p f^{(q)}(\xi_2)$
* $T = F\left(\frac{h}{4}\right) + \frac{C}{4^p} h^p f^{(q)}(\xi_3)$


$$T = \underbrace{\frac{2^p F\left(\frac{h}{2}\right) - F(h)}{2^p - 1}}_{F_1(h)} - \mathbf{\frac{3C}{8(2^p - 1)} h^{p+2} f^{(q+2)}(\xi_1^*)}$$
  $$T = \underbrace{\frac{2^p F\left(\frac{h}{4}\right) - F\left(\frac{h}{2}\right)}{2^p - 1}}_{F_1(h/2)} - \mathbf{\frac{3C}{8(2^p - 1)} \left(\frac{h}{2}\right)^{p+2} f^{(q+2)}(\xi_2^*)}$$

$\mathbf{f^{(q+2)}(\xi_1^*) - f^{(q+2)}(\xi_2^*) = \frac{3}{8} h^2 f^{(q+4)}(\xi^{**})}$

$$
T = \frac{2^{p+2} F_1\left(\frac{h}{2}\right) - F_1(h)}{2^{p+2} - 1} + \mathbf{\frac{9C}{64(2^p - 1)(2^{p+2} - 1)} h^{p+4} f^{(q+4)}(\xi^{**})}
$$
$$
\mathbf{T = \frac{2^{2p+2} F\left(\frac{h}{4}\right) - (2^{p+2} + 2^p) F\left(\frac{h}{2}\right) + F(h)}{(2^p - 1)(2^{p+2} - 1)} + \frac{9C}{64(2^p - 1)(2^{p+2} - 1)} h^{p+4} f^{(q+4)}(\xi^{**} \mid (c-h, c+h))}
$$


# 리처드슨 외삽법의 $m$단계 일반화 (Generalization)

초기값: $F_0(h) = F(h)$ 로 둘 때, $m$단계 외삽값 $F_m(h)$는 다음과 같이 정의된다 ($m = 1, 2, 3, \dots$).
$$
\mathbf{F_m(h) = \frac{2^{p + 2(m-1)} F_{m-1}\left(\frac{h}{2}\right) - F_{m-1}(h)}{2^{p + 2(m-1)} - 1} = F_{m-1}\left(\frac{h}{2}\right) + \frac{F_{m-1}\left(\frac{h}{2}\right) - F_{m-1}(h)}{2^{p + 2(m-1)} - 1}}
$$

외삽을 $m$번 거칠 때마다:
1. **부호:** $(-1)^m$ (플러스/마이너스 번갈아 등장)
2. **계수 비율:** $\left(\frac{3}{8}\right)^m$ 이 곱해짐
3. **분모의 연립 인수:** $\prod_{j=0}^{m-1} \left(2^{p+2j} - 1\right)$ 로 누적 곱
4. **오차 차수:** $h^p \to \mathbf{h^{p+2m}}$
5. **도함수 차수:** $f^{(q)} \to \mathbf{f^{(q+2m)}}$
$$
\mathbf{T = F_m(h) + (-1)^m \left(\frac{3}{8}\right)^m \left( \prod_{j=0}^{m-1} \frac{1}{2^{p+2j} - 1} \right) C \, h^{p+2m} f^{(q+2m)}\big(\xi^{(m)} \mid (c-h, c+h)\big)}
$$

* $m=1$ (1단계 외삽, $h/2$ 사용)
  $$T = F_1(h) \mathbf{- \frac{3C}{8(2^p - 1)} h^{p+2} f^{(q+2)}(\xi^*)}$$

* $m=2$ (2단계 외삽, $h/4$ 사용)
  $$T = F_2(h) \mathbf{+ \frac{9C}{64(2^p - 1)(2^{p+2} - 1)} h^{p+4} f^{(q+4)}(\xi^{**})}$$

* $m=3$ (3단계 외삽, $h/8$ 사용)
  $$T = F_3(h) \mathbf{- \frac{27C}{512(2^p - 1)(2^{p+2} - 1)(2^{p+4} - 1)} h^{p+6} f^{(q+6)}(\xi^{***})}$$
